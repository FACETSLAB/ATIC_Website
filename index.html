Who We Are

Sanjay Goel, PhD

Director at ATIC

Sanjay Goel is the Morris Mossey Endowed Professor and Chair of Information Security and Digital Forensics at the University at Albany. His work focuses on cybersecurity, AI, accessibility innovation, and community-centered technology research. Through interdisciplinary partnerships, he leads research and educational programs to support responsible technology development, advance solutions, and real-world impact across diverse communities.
