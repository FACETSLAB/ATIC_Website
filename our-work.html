Our Work

Our research focuses on AI, accessibility, assistive technology, neurodiversity, XR, adaptive learning, and mental health innovation. We develop and evaluate human-centered technologies that support inclusive learning, accessibility, communication, digital participation, and community engagement.

Neurodiversity-Aware Curriculum Optimization

Lead: Yujung Hwang

Research on neurodiversity-aware adaptive curriculum systems, accessibility-centered AI, and personalized learning environments.

Research

XR for IDD Interventions

Lead: Jingyi (Jenny) Huang, PhD

AI-powered XR interventions supporting social interaction, stress management, and job-readiness training for individuals with IDD.

Research

Assistive Technology Hub

Lead: Yujung Hwang

Development of the NYS Assistive Technology Hub to improve accessibility, resource navigation, and community access to assistive technology services, organizations, and support systems across New York State.

Research

Esports-Based Accessibility Interventions

Lead: Lakshika Vaishnav

Research on structured esports and game-based interventions designed to support executive functioning, social interaction, accessibility, teamwork, and cognitive skill development for neurodiverse and disability communities.

Research

Mental Health AI Systems

Lead: Lakshika Vaishnav, Jaiganesh Anandan

Research on AI-driven mental health support systems, including longitudinal mental health tracking, explainable depression and PTSD detection, safety-aware AI architectures, and clinically grounded multimodal AI technologies.
