Our Work

Our research focuses on AI, accessibility, assistive technology, neurodiversity, XR, adaptive learning, and mental health innovation. We develop and evaluate human-centered technologies that support inclusive learning, accessibility, communication, digital participation, and community engagement.

Lead: Yujung Hwang

Neurodiversity-Aware Curriculum Optimization

An AI-powered adaptive curriculum learning system that creates personalized education for neurodivergent individuals with adaptive content and learning pathways.

Mental Health AI Systems

Research on responsible AI systems for mental health support, including PTSD and depression detection using clinically grounded multi-modal AI approaches, as well as fine-tuned LLMs for mental health support using text and audio modalities.

The 21st Annual Symposium on Information Assurance (ASIA '26)

The 21st Annual Symposium on Information Assurance (ASIA '26) will be held in conjunction with the 28th New York State Cyber Security Conference (NYSCSC) June 2 to 3, 2026. This joint event will attract over 3,200 participants, including practitioners, researchers and vendors.
