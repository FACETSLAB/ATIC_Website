Our Work

Our research focuses on AI, accessibility, assistive technology, neurodiversity, XR, adaptive learning, and mental health innovation. We develop and evaluate human-centered technologies that support inclusive learning, accessibility, communication, digital participation, and community engagement.

2025 DREAM Symposium

Event

The DREAM Event, hosted by the Office of the Chief Disability Officer (CDO) and New York State partners, brought together community members, employers, educators, researchers, and accessibility advocates to promote disability inclusion.

The 21st Annual Symposium on Information Assurance (ASIA '26)

The 21st Annual Symposium on Information Assurance (ASIA '26) will be held in conjunction with the 28th New York State Cyber Security Conference (NYSCSC) June 2 to 3, 2026. This joint event will attract over 3,200 participants, including practitioners, researchers and vendors.

The Symposium is organized by the Massry School of Business at the University at Albany, State University of New York, the Chief Information Technology Services (ITS) and the New York State Forum. Through generous sponsorship, we have free registration for all presenters, authors of accepted papers and members of government organizations. Special themes for this year's conference are threats from artificial intelligence, auditing artificial Intelligence and using artificial intelligence for cybersecurity and digital forensics.
