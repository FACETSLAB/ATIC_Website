ATIC Testing Laboratory

The ATIC Testing Laboratory conducts structured evaluations of assistive and adaptive technologies across real-world environments including home, workplace, healthcare, and community settings.

Evaluation Process

1

Product Intake & Documentation Review

Reviewing product purpose, technical specifications, setup requirements, accessibility features, and intended user populations.

2

Structured Usability Testing

Evaluating ease of use, learning curve, setup complexity, and integration into everyday activities.

3

Real-World Assessment

Testing product performance across home, workplace, healthcare, and community environments.

4

Accessibility & Independence Evaluation

Assessing whether the technology supports accessibility, reduces barriers, and improves meaningful independence.

5

Reliability & Connectivity Assessment

Examining durability, consistency of performance, maintenance needs, and connectivity requirements.

6

Findings & Public Reporting

Publishing structured evaluation summaries, observed limitations, best-fit user profiles, and evidence-based findings through the ATIC website.
